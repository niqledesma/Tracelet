Supported formats and limitations
  • PDF: extracts selectable text and runs OCR on pages without readable text.
  • Images: OCR can read visible words in common picture formats. It does not describe every object in a picture.
  • DOCX: extracts document text. Pictures embedded in documents are not OCR'd in this version.
  • PPTX/XLSX: extracts some text from slides and spreadsheet cells. Complex formatting, charts and images may be missed.
  • Other files: attempts text decoding, but selecting a binary file does not make its contents readable.
  • Semantic search: downloads a language model and may be slow or unsupported in some mobile browsers.
  • Deleted files: Tracelet cannot recover deleted data or automatically scan the entire device.
